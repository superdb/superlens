    Your data stays in your browser

    SuperLens runs SuperDB, compiled to WebAssembly, inside this browser tab. Files you open or drop are read directly by the browser and queried in memory. They are never uploaded, and SuperLens has no server that could receive them.

    The only things this page downloads are its own code and the query engine, from this site. Its content security policy blocks connections to any other site.

    Closing or reloading the tab discards everything.
